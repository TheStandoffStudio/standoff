GUNS, AMMO AND ABILITIES

Your loadout has 4 slots, one per category: 1 Primary, 2 Secondary, 3 Utility and 4 Melee (set them in Armory > Loadout). Each category has 11 items to unlock. Every gun has a magazine and 100 bullets per round in total (the loaded magazine counts). Ammo refills at the start of every new round, so spend it wisely. Guns reload automatically when empty, or press R. Utility items are gadgets, not weapons (riot shield, medkit, grenades, turrets and more): click to use them, and they recharge every round. Melee weapons use no bullets: click to swing. Every gun has a signature ability (burn, freeze, chain lightning, lifesteal and more). The price of a gun decides its rarity: pricier guns hit harder, reload faster and have stronger abilities.

COMBAT TOOLS (FREE, RECHARGE EVERY SECTOR)

  • Arc Surge [G] - instant lightning strikes up to 5 hostiles near you. It cannot miss.
  • EMP Pulse [F] - wipes enemy bullets, freezes nearby hostiles and cancels their items and shields.

FIELD SUPPLIES (BOUGHT WITH CREDITS)

Grenade Turret, Proximity Mines, Combat Stim, Airstrike Beacon and Bubble Shield. Buy them in Armory > Supplies, cycle with E, deploy with Q. Your stock carries between runs.

HOSTILES

Every hostile carries its own gun. Smarter ones dodge your shots, hide behind barriers, throw frags, drop cover, use medkits and stims and put up shields. The higher the difficulty, the more often they do it. EMP Pulse and explosives beat shields.

CREDITS AND INTEL

Credits buy medkits, field supplies and weapon cases. Intel unlocks guns. Intel is rare: elites, Overlords, daily missions, ranks, awards and career ops all pay it. Expect around four hours of play to afford the strongest gun.

RANKS, AWARDS AND OPERATIONS

Every rank pays Credits and Intel (claim them in Ranks). Trophies and achievements have rarities and pay more the rarer they are. Operations has daily and weekly missions plus ten long-term career ops.

SKINS AND DROPS

Each weapon case drops skins for one gun. Any gun can roll any rarity, from Common to Mythic. Equipped skins show on your gun, your bullets and the HUD.

DIFFICULTY

0.5x Recruit, 1x Standard, 2x Veteran, 3x Nightmare. Higher difficulty means tougher, faster hostiles with smarter tactics, and bigger rewards.

SAVING

Progress is not saved until you press Save (main menu, pause menu or Settings). You can switch on Autosave in Settings. The game warns you if you try to leave with unsaved progress.
